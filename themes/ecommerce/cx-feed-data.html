  • Validation: flags missing IDs, titles, descriptions, prices or image URLs.
  • Image URL: should be publicly reachable and point to the correct product image.

  • Live upload: check the selected platform's current feed specification before uploading.
Demo environment: this tool exports the products in data/products.js. It does not connect to WordPress, sync automatically or upload anything to Meta/Google.
